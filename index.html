Three website options

Same content and the same photos in all three — only the layout changes. Have a look on a phone as well as a computer, since most people will find the site on a phone.

  • A

    Classic

    Logo front and centre, services in cards, everything down the middle. The most familiar, straightforward version.

    Open option A
  • B

    Bold

    Dark top half with a photo of you working, services in a numbered list, photos on a black background, phone number big and red at the bottom.

    Open option B
  • C

    Clean

    A menu bar fixed down the left with your phone number always on screen, lots of white space, photos in a tighter grid.

    Open option C

On a computer, some tiles in the photo section are marked Before — hovering swaps them to the finished job, and clicking opens both side by side. On a phone those tiles just show the finished job.
